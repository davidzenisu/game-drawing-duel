- Prompts:
	- Different themes that will show up as backgrounds or potential predemermined special effects (see below)
- Sketch:
	- Hurries: once a day, each player can send a free hurry to another player to cut their drawing time. A hurry sent today hits the target's challenger drawing **the next day**, so it can be sent from day 1 on (in speed mode on days 1 to 3, since drawings are on days 2 to 4). The target is informed while drawing (and not from the start) so to surprise them and intentionally force more crude paintings. It takes away a share of the drawing time left at a random moment, and every hurry a player receives counts.
- Duplicates:
	- Any duplicates provide "upgrades" for special effects. These includes shadows and light effects (different elements?)
	- Should be done via a skill tree
	- Different rarities should have different trees.
	- A character's upgrades show wherever one of your own characters appears: the collection, the fighter picker, fights, the summon reveal (where a duplicate that pays for the next upgrade says "Upgrade ready!") and the pool of what you can pull. Challengers show plain.
	- General idea: Different "classes" for different roles?
