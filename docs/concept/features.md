- Prompts:
	- Different themes that will show up as backgrounds or potential predemermined special effects (see below)
- Sketch:
	- Hurries: once a day, each player can send a free hurry to another player to cut their drawing time. A hurry sent today hits the target's challenger drawing **the next day**, so it can be sent from day 1 on (in speed mode on days 1 to 3, since drawings are on days 2 to 4). The target is informed while drawing (and not from the start) so to surprise them and intentionally force more crude paintings. It takes away 5 seconds of drawing time, whenever it hits, and every hurry a player receives counts. Hurries never come too late: they hit with between 55 and 15 seconds left, spread evenly over that stretch in the order they were sent, each at a random moment within its share of it (with many hurries, several can hit at once). When sending it, the player can add an optional short message of up to 60 characters (the quick picks "No pressure 😇", "You can do it 💪" and "Hurry crew 👩‍🚀" fill it in), and sees it again on the hub's hurry button. Messages stay hidden until their hurry hits: then a banner over the top bar, beside the clock and never over the canvas, names the sender, the time taken and their message ("Robin: No pressure 😇"); when several hit at once, their messages play one after another. The drawing's result screen names everyone who sent one, with their messages, in the order they hit. Speed mode works the same.
