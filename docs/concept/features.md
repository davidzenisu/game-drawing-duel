- Prompts:
	- Different themes that will show up as backgrounds or potential predemermined special effects (see below)
- Sketch:
	- Hurries: once a day, each player can send a free hurry to another player to cut their drawing time. A hurry sent today hits the target's challenger drawing **the next day**, so it can be sent from day 1 on (in speed mode on days 1 to 3, since drawings are on days 2 to 4). The target is informed while drawing (and not from the start) so to surprise them and intentionally force more crude paintings. It takes away 10% of the drawing time left (at least 2 seconds), and every hurry a player receives counts. Hurries never come too late: they hit with between 55 and 15 seconds left, spread out over that stretch in the order they were sent, each at a random moment within its share of it.
- Duplicates:
	- Any duplicates provide "upgrades" for special effects. These includes shadows and light effects (different elements?)
	- Should be done via a skill tree
	- Different rarities should have different trees.
	- A character's upgrades show in the collection, the fighter picker and fights. Summons and the pool of what you can pull show characters plain, so the drawing stays the focus; a summoned duplicate that pays for the next upgrade says "Upgrade ready!". Challengers show plain.
	- General idea: Different "classes" for different roles?
